Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This website

pachamamavalleyfarm.com doesn't use accounts, cookies for tracking, or advertising. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs to run and protect the service.

The live bee yard video is played either from YouTube or from our own camera feed. When the YouTube player is shown, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to it; we use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) player.

If you email us

If you write to info@pachamamavalleyfarm.com, we use your email only to reply to you. We don't sell or share it.

The bee cam's YouTube connection

The farm runs a small program that keeps our own YouTube live stream running. It signs in to our own YouTube channel only, and uses YouTube's API to check whether the stream is live and to restart it when needed. It doesn't access anyone else's data, and nothing it handles is shared with anyone.
