# Pachamama Valley Farms logo

Created October 22, 2026. It replaces the earlier badge, whose arched text ran into the bee and whose stars cut through letters.

## What it is

The logo is a round badge with two parts.

- **Outer ring (dark pine green):** "PACHAMAMA VALLEY FARMS" arches across the top and "LOCAL TEXAS HONEY" runs along the bottom, both in cream. Two cream stars sit in the gaps between them.
- **Inner scene:** an orange sunburst with a pale sun, a honeybee centered in the sun, and green hills. Three honeycomb cells with a single honey drip sit on the hills.

There is also a **simplified mark**, which is the inner scene with no text. Use it where the full badge would be too small to read.

## Files

| `pvf-logo-2000.png` | 2000 px, transparent background | Large digital use, presentations |

The SVG and PDF are true vector files. All lettering has been converted to outlines, so they don't need any fonts installed and they look identical everywhere.

## Typeface

The lettering is **Oswald Bold** (SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use). Use Oswald Bold for any matching text, such as jar labels or signs. The website uses Bricolage Grotesque and Source Serif 4. Those fonts pair well with the badge but are not part of it.

## Usage

- **Minimum size:** use the full badge at 96 px or larger (about 1 inch in print). Below that, use the simplified mark.
- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the badge equal to at least one-tenth of its diameter.
- **Backgrounds:** the badge works on white, cream, photos and dark green.
- **Don't:**
  - stretch it or squash it out of round
  - recolor parts of it
  - add drop shadows other than a soft one over photos
  - put text across it
